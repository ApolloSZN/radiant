## Objective
Move Radiant from progress-curve analysis into a consequential real production system without fabricating quantitative facts from qualitative reports.

## Evidence consulted
DOE 2024 Large Power Transformer Resilience Report; DOE 2022 Electric Grid Supply Chain Report; DOE 2014 LPT study. Primary-source claims are encoded in `data/transformers/evidence.csv`.

## Build
Added `radiant/engines/constraint_resolution.py`, `radiant/data/transformer_case.py`, transformer evidence CSV, and four tests. The engine maximizes output subject to process and material constraints, then computes marginal throughput from +20% relaxations. Evidence IDs bind claims/parameters to provenance.

## Quantitative output
The initial network uses normalized scenario coefficients, explicitly NOT empirical U.S. capacities. Baseline qualified-LPT throughput = 0.72 normalized units. +20% test/qualification capacity => 0.78 (+8.33%); then assembly binds. All other single +20% relaxations have zero marginal gain while testing remains binding.

## Negative/guardrail result
DOE reports GOES vulnerability/import dependence, but this is insufficient to assert GOES is the current marginal throughput bottleneck. Radiant therefore refuses to convert that prose into a fake capacity number. The current intervention ranking validates the engine only.
