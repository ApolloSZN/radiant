### 1. GOES embodied in imported laminations and cores, 2019
- Evidence ID: `COMMERCE2020_SECTION232_GOES_EMBODIED_CORES_2019`
- Value: **68.0 kt GOES-equivalent**
- Period: calendar 2019
- Publication date used for the cited public source: 2021-11-18
- Evidence class: `federal_report_estimate_from_industry_weight_estimate`
- Scope/provenance: Commerce states that core trade is collected in **units, not weight**; the 68,000 metric ton estimate uses Core Coalition public-comment weight estimates.
