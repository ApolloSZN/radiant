# Run 023 — v0.14-rc11

## Starting state
v0.14-rc10 / Run 022; 84 tests green; release 9/11.

## Objective
Search for compatible transformer product/material evidence and strengthen empirical production-network parameterization without converting planning assumptions into measurements.

## Sources consulted
- National Laboratory of the Rockies (DOE-funded), January 2026, *Informing Transmission Supply Chain Needs from National Transmission Studies*, NLR/TP-6A40-97167.
- Siemens Energy Charlotte expansion evidence retained from prior runs.
- USITC/DOE LPT physical characteristics retained from prior runs.

## Implementation
Added a typed NLR 2026 reference material model, deterministic material-demand calculation, national modeled-scenario evidence, and Charlotte product-MVA sensitivity scenarios. The model preserves source epistemics: `published_model_assumption` and `modeled_national_planning_scenario` are not promoted to measured factory facts.

## Quantitative outputs
- NLR reference: ~600 kg/MVA total LPT mass for >100 MVA analytical scope.
- Assumed material shares: 48% GOES, 30% copper, 12% non-GOES steel, 10% other.
- Implied reference coefficients: 288 kg GOES/MVA and 180 kg copper/MVA.
- For 57 Charlotte units, explicit 100–700 MVA sensitivity scenarios imply 1.642–11.491 million kg GOES/year; this is not an actual Charlotte demand claim because product mix is unknown.
- NLR modeled national scenarios require ~1,370–1,510 transformers/year and total transmission expansion requires 94–114 thousand tons GOES/year and 61–74 thousand tons copper/year. NLR reports GOES demand exceeds 45% of 2019–2023 apparent consumption.

## Negative results / rejected approaches
- Did not infer Charlotte MVA mix from the factory's 57-unit target.
- Did not treat NLR's 600 kg/MVA or material shares as measured BOM coefficients.
- Did not use national planning demand as evidence of actual national production capacity.
